Getting around by campus bus

Rutgers runs a free campus bus system connecting Busch campus, where the conference is held, with the College Avenue campus and the rest of the university. The buses are open to everyone, with no fare or ID needed, so you can simply board.

  • Most conference sessions are on Busch campus (Allison Road Classrooms, SEC, and Weeks Hall) and are within easy walking distance of each other. See the Campus Map.
  • The meeting runs over a weekend, so buses follow the weekend routes (Weekend 1 and Weekend 2). Weekend service is less frequent than weekdays, so it helps to check departure times ahead.
Download the TripShot app. It shows live bus locations, lets you plan a trip between any two stops, and lists full campus schedules:
ipo.rutgers.edu/transportation/new-transit-tracking-tripshot
